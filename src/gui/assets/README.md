`be-quiet-logo.png` is the transparent white logo from the be quiet! logo
archive supplied by the user (`be_quiet_logo.zip`, Online/png/Inclusive
registered sign). It identifies the Light Mount chassis in the per-key editor.
The original image is embedded without alterations.

`mdi-volume-low.svg` and `mdi-cancel.svg` are the original Material Design Icons
from https://github.com/Templarian/MaterialDesign-SVG (retrieved 2026-10-07).
Their license is included in `MDI-LICENSE.txt`. They identify the media wheel
functions and are tinted when drawn to match the key legend color.
